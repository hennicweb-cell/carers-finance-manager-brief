About the organisation

A carer-centred charity, run well, going somewhere

Carers Support Bristol & South Gloucestershire exists to support adult and young carers across Bristol and South Gloucestershire who are, or were, caring for someone who is disabled, elderly or ill — through practical help, advice, advocacy and support. Around 50 staff and 9 trustees deliver services funded mainly by local authority and health commissioners, with government grants and contracts making up roughly three-quarters of income.

The charity holds the Carers Trust "centre of excellence" designation and the NCVO Level 2 Trusted Charity Mark, and its trustees' reports consistently emphasise listening and learning, valuing people, and ensuring carers' own voices shape governance and service design.

"A better life for carers of all ages, where they are recognised, valued and supported in their caring role and empowered to fulfil their aspirations." — organisational vision statement
Where the organisation is now

A charity mid-merger, with the hard groundwork already done

Like much of the carers sector, the charity has been staff-heavy and increasingly squeezed as costs have risen faster than income. After several years of the idea being on the table, the trustees have committed to a merger with Bath Carers. Over the coming year, Bath Carers will initially take on Bristol's services under a contract management arrangement while the formal merger — including the changes to governing documents needed to operate beyond Bristol and South Gloucestershire — is worked through behind the scenes.

Once merged, the enlarged charity expects to extend into North Somerset and to be well placed to tender for wider West of England Combined Authority carers provision, tracking the same consolidation already under way among local hospital trusts. Neither organisation currently employs a qualified accountant — finance has run through a Finance Manager and an external bookkeeper — and building that capability, in an accountant with genuine charity-sector fund accounting knowledge, is a specific priority of this hire.

The good news for whoever joins: a year of work by the CEO has already untangled a backlog of restricted-fund reconciliation issues that had let some contracts run over their funded value unnoticed. The ledger (in QuickBooks, used by both Carers Support BSG and Bath Carers) is now in materially better shape. This role is about incremental improvement and keeping it that way — not a root-and-branch clean-up.

The role

What you'll own

Taken from the charity's job description (June 2026). You'll lead the finance function day to day, working closely with the Chief Executive and Treasurer, and line-managing a Finance Officer who handles payroll support and transactional finance.

1Management accounts & reporting

  • Produce accurate monthly management accounts — income & expenditure, balance sheet reconciliations, variance analysis — for the CEO, Treasurer, Board and Finance Sub-Committee
  • Maintain records of restricted and unrestricted funding
  • Prepare the annual audit file and lead liaison with external auditors

2Cash flow, investments & reserves

  • Produce short, medium and long-term cash flow forecasts, flagging funding gaps or surpluses early
  • Review investment and deposit options so cash and reserves earn the best return within the charity's risk appetite, reporting performance to the Treasurer and Board
  • Maintain and monitor the reserves policy

3Payroll & line management

  • Line manage the Finance Officer — supervision, appraisal and development
  • Jointly oversee payroll: BACS data, pension auto-enrolment, PAYE and NI
  • Ensure invoices, expenses, banking and petty cash follow the financial controls policy, with clear segregation of duties

4Policies, systems & compliance

  • Review and maintain financial policies, procedures and systems as the charity's needs evolve
  • Lead selection and development of financial and related IT systems, including accounting software and CharityLog CRM financial reporting
  • Ensure compliance with Companies House, Charity Commission, Gift Aid, VAT and data protection requirements

5Budgets, bids & board support

  • Lead the annual planning and budgeting process, built on the Full Cost Recovery model
  • Support the fundraising and senior management team with costings, forecasting and modelling for bids, grants and tenders
  • Prepare reports for the Board and Finance Sub-Committee, and help non-finance budget holders understand and manage their budgets
